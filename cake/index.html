The BEST Enterprise AI Transformation Recipe

Looking for a foolproof Enterprise AI transformation recipe your C-suite will love? You’ve come to the right place! We’re serving up old-fashioned flavor, mom-and-pop charm, and absolutely no skimping on the good stuff.

This tried-and-tested recipe combines powerful infrastructure with the freshest enterprise data, a generous helping of context, and just the right amount of agent orchestration. We’ve spent years perfecting the ingredients and method for this award-winning dish – and now we’re finally sharing the recipe with you.

Best of all? It comes together using ingredients you already have. Simply combine, orchestrate, and bake until your Enterprise AI is golden brown.

What you need to know about this Enterprise AI recipe

  • Serves: the entire enterprise
  • Prep time: depends how organized your data is
  • Cooking time: quicker than than your last AI transformation program
  • Difficulty: easier when everyone agrees what Enterprise AI means
  • Structured process data, fresh and long-life
  • Unstructured process data, to taste
  • Business knowledge, generously portioned
  • Decision intelligence, for added zing
  • Strategic objectives, thinly sliced
  • Guardrails, as required
  • AI, obviously
  • A process intelligence platform
  • Your existing data infrastructure
  • A real-time digital twin, preheated
  • The AI tools and agents of your choice
  • A cross-functional team of chefs

Step 1: The base layer: Process Data

Give your Enterprise AI transformation its distinctive flavor

Bring together raw data from across...

...your business in your high-performance data infrastructure. For best results, use the freshest data available.

Weigh out your structured data first: things like...

...orders, invoices and deliveries from your ERP, CRM and supply chain systems. Measure carefully: this is what gives the base its structure.

Add unstructured data – the more you add...

...the richer the flavor. Emails, tickets, documents and chats all bring texture your structured data can’t.

Stir until every step, interaction, and decision that...

...makes up your processes is evenly mixed into one connected view of how your business really runs.

Set aside briefly as you move to step two...

...your base layer will keep refreshing itself while you prepare the operational context.

  • Chef’s tip

    If you’ve already cleaned and prepped your raw data, zero-copy integrations let you access it directly from its Databricks or Snowflake containers. No bowl scraping required.

Step 2: Middle layer: Operational context

Provide Enterprise AI clarity with a layer of gooey goodness

This middle bit is our favorite and guaranteed to …

... get the C-suite asking for more.. Operational context gives your Enterprise AI the clarity it needs to grow and delight. Skip this step and you risk serving up AI with operational blind spots, half-baked answers, and decisions nobody ordered. Start by preheating your real-time digital twin (or context model) to operating temperature.

In a separate bowl, whip up your business …

… knowledge: policies, rules, KPIs and the know-how that lives in your teams’ heads, until it forms soft peaks.

Now combine your process data and …

… business knowledge, folding gently so your AI understands not only what happened, but why.

For extra zing, gently fold in your other …

… sources of context: benchmarks, external signals and the goals you’ve set for the business.

With process intelligence and decision …

… intelligence in the mix, your middle layer is ready to hold up everything you put on top.

Step 3: Making it special: The AI frosting

Get creative putting those agents into action

Short on time? Reach for oven-ready AI solutions …

... and platforms from industry leaders like Anthropic, Microsoft, Databricks, and Amazon. Simply remove from packaging, spread them over your operational context middle layer, and serve. Mix and match solutions at will until you hit the sweet spot.

Cooking something special? Make your own …

… agents and apps on top of your process intelligence, seasoned exactly to your business’s taste.

Pipe it, layer it, brûlée it. You’re limited only by your …

… imagination. Every agent draws on the same context, so the flavors always work together.

Once everything’s assembled, allow your agents …

… to act on what they see, with guardrails in place, and watch the value rise.

And there you have it:

Enterprise AI that understands how your business actually works and can turn that understanding into value.

Serve immediately across the enterprise.

It’s flexible, scalable, and suitable for enterprises of every shape and size.

Pairs well with: ambitious transformation goals, measurable business outcomes, and a nice crisp RoAI.

Leftovers: unlikely. Your stakeholders will be back for more.

This might just be your favorite recipe yet!
